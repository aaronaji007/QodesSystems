# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web (Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile; optimized for enterprise procurement, CTOs, and banking executives).

## Stack
Next.js 15 (App Router, React 18) + Tailwind CSS + PostgreSQL (Vercel Postgres / Neon) + Lucide Icons + Radix UI Primitives.

## Users
- **Banking Executives & CTOs**: Leaders evaluating enterprise core banking replacements, modernization of Temenos T24 / SAP Core Banking stacks, and cloud CBS migrations.
- **Financial Risk & Compliance Officers**: Security teams evaluating APRA CPS 234 compliance, penetration testing, ICT environment audits, source code security, and vulnerability assessments.
- **Enterprise Engineering Leads**: Technology teams seeking specialized staff augmentation and quality engineering for mission-critical banking transformations.
- **Candidates / Banking Engineers**: Senior CBS engineers, SAP banking consultants, and cybersecurity professionals seeking roles.

## Product Purpose
A premier enterprise technology & security platform for Qodes Systems (Melbourne, Australia & Global) that establishes institutional credibility, showcases proprietary AI-driven Core Banking System (CBS) capabilities alongside SAP and Temenos T24 implementation expertise, and captures high-value enterprise RFPs, consultation inquiries, and career applications into a secure PostgreSQL database.

## Positioning
An elite banking technology advisory and CBS engineering powerhouse that bridges decades of deep enterprise core banking experience with modern cybersecurity and AI orchestration. Distinct from generic web consultancies or off-the-shelf SaaS by virtue of mission-critical banking domain expertise, APRA regulatory adherence, and Tier-1 implementation pedigree.

## Operating Context
- Public corporate web experience spanning solutions, banking products, cybersecurity compliance, and corporate reach.
- Direct contact and advisory booking workflow with immediate validation and live PostgreSQL persistence.
- Career portal with resume document intake and automated notification dispatch.
- Admin management interface for tracking client inquiries and prospective engineering candidates.

## Brand Commitments
- **Institutional Authority**: Uncompromising aesthetic restraint — clean, high-contrast, structured, and calm.
- **Security-First**: Zero plaintext credentials, end-to-end encrypted storage, and clean architectural separation.
- **Performance & Precision**: Sub-second load times, smooth micro-interactions without visual noise, and zero layout shift.

## Product Principles
- **Clarity Over Clutter**: Present complex core banking capabilities through structured bento architectures and clear technical hierarchies rather than bloated marketing jargon.
- **Tactile Precision**: Every interactive element offers subtle, purposeful feedback (focus states, gentle scale transforms, mouse-aware spotlights).
- **Domain Authenticity**: True Australian and global banking context (APRA CPS 234 compliance, Tier-1 core banking migrations, SWIFT/ISO 20022 readiness).
